Native Prairie Veterans Foundation provides fully funded outdoor fellowship programs for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families through hunting, conservation, and community connection.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. EIN: 42-2391986. Belton, Missouri.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, legally separate from Native Prairie Outfitters LLC, a for-profit business. Both are led by Steven Vaca. Foundation donations remain Foundation assets — any payment to the Outfitter requires documented fair-value services and disinterested board approval.
