Privacy Policy

Effective November 12, 2026. This policy explains what information Native Prairie Veterans Foundation collects through this website, how it is used, and the choices you have.

Who we are

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 42-2391986), located at 214 W North Ave, Belton, MO 64012. We run fully funded outdoor fellowship programs for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families. This website is nativeprairieveteransfoundation.org. Native Prairie Outfitters LLC is a separate for-profit business; this policy covers information collected through this website, including its Outfitters pages.

Information we collect

We collect information in only a few ways. When you email or call us, we receive the information you share with us, such as your name, email address, phone number, and what you tell us about yourself or your inquiry. When you donate online, your donation is processed by Zeffy, a third-party donation platform. Zeffy collects your payment information directly; we receive your name, email address, and donation amount from Zeffy, and we never see or store your card number. This website does not offer user accounts, does not run analytics or advertising trackers, and does not serve third-party ads. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs, such as the pages requested and the time of the request, to operate and secure the site.

How we use information

We use the information we receive to answer your questions, arrange fellowships and volunteer opportunities, acknowledge donations, keep the records a nonprofit is required to keep, and operate this website. We use donation information only for the purposes of the donation and the acknowledgment that follows it.

What we do not do with information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We do not share it with other organizations for their own marketing. We share it only when needed to run the Foundation, for example with Zeffy to process a donation, or when required by law.

Children's privacy

This website is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has shared personal information with us, contact us and we will delete it.

Your choices

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing steven@nativeprairieveteransfoundation.org or calling 816-585-3699. We will respond to reasonable requests. Some information, such as donation records, must be kept for legal and accounting reasons even after other information is deleted.

Links to other sites

This website links to other sites, including our Facebook page and the Zeffy donation page. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and this policy does not cover them. Please review the privacy policy of any site you visit through a link from here.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the effective date above and post the revised policy on this page.

Contact us

If you have questions about this policy, email steven@nativeprairieveteransfoundation.org, call 816-585-3699, or write to Native Prairie Veterans Foundation, 214 W North Ave, Belton, MO 64012.
