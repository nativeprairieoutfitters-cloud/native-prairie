Five ways to give

1. Give online through Hunt for a Hero

Online donations are processed through Zeffy, which states that 100% of the donation goes to the mission.

Donate now

2. Become a founding partner

Founding partners provide the resources to expand NPVF outdoor fellowship opportunities:

  • Shoveler Partner — $1,000
  • Redhead Partner — $2,500
  • Bluebill Partner — $5,000
  • Canada Goose Partner — $10,000

3. Run a peer fundraiser

Create your own fundraising effort and bring friends, family, and community together behind the mission.

4. Donate gear or services

In-kind donations of gear or services help create outdoor fellowship opportunities. If you have resources that may help, we would like to hear from you.

5. Planned or legacy giving

A planned gift can help support the future of NPVF and its mission of serving veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations may be tax-deductible as allowed by law.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation provides fully funded outdoor fellowship programs for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families through hunting, conservation, and community connection.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. EIN: 42-2391986. Belton, Missouri.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, legally separate from Native Prairie Outfitters LLC, a for-profit business. Both are led by Steven Vaca. Foundation donations remain Foundation assets — any payment to the Outfitter requires documented fair-value services and disinterested board approval.
