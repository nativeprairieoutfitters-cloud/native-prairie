Built for those who served.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri nonprofit corporation, recognized as a 501(c)(3) public charity.

Our founder, Steven Vaca, started NPVF around a simple belief: time outdoors, done right, helps people heal and connect.

Our mission

Fully funded outdoor hunting and conservation experiences for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families.

Our impact so far

NPVF has served five veterans through fully funded fellowships since our founding in 2026, and our goal is to serve twenty veterans by the end of 2026 and forty in 2027. Every fellowship is fully funded: participants pay nothing for the hunt, the guiding, or the ground. A single veteran's hunt day costs about $250, a hunt weekend about $500, and a full four-hunter hunt day about $1,500. Those are the real costs your donation covers.

What a fellowship looks like

A fellowship starts before first light, with a safety briefing and a plan for the day from our hunt-day Safety Officer. We hunt on private partner land in Cass County, Missouri, always with the landowner's written permission. We provide the guiding, the ground, the decoys, and the calling. The fellowship does not end when the hunt does: the shared time before, during, and after every outing is the point, and past participants are invited back to hunt again, mentor newcomers, and help lead the next fellowship.

How the work is funded

NPVF is funded by individual donations through our Hunt for a Hero campaign, grants from foundations and companies, and fundraising raffles. Every dollar supports fully funded outdoor fellowships. We keep our costs low by relying on donated gear, partner land, and volunteers, so more of each gift goes directly to getting veterans outdoors.

Two separate organizations

Native Prairie Outfitters LLC is a separate for-profit business. It is not part of NPVF. Donations to NPVF remain NPVF assets; any payment by NPVF to the Outfitter requires documented fair-value services, disinterested board approval, and separate books.
