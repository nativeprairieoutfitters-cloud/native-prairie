Frequently asked questions

Straight answers about our programs, donations, and how to get involved.

Who can participate in NPVF programs?

NPVF provides fully funded outdoor fellowship programs for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families. Our programs create opportunities for hunting, conservation work, and community connection.

Is participation really free?

Yes. Every NPVF program is fully funded — there is no cost for participants. Our goal is to remove barriers and create meaningful outdoor experiences for those we serve.

Do I need hunting experience to participate?

No prior hunting experience is required. NPVF programs include guided experiences where participants can take part regardless of experience level.

What gear is provided?

NPVF works to make participation accessible; specific gear availability depends on each program and partnership.

Where do NPVF hunts and programs happen?

NPVF is based in Belton, Missouri, in the Kansas City region. Programs take place on private partner land through available locations and partnerships.

How do I apply to participate?

Contact us to learn about upcoming fellowship opportunities and the application process.

Can Gold Star families participate?

Yes. Gold Star families are included among the participants NPVF serves.

Where does my donation go?

Donations support fully funded outdoor fellowship programs. Verified program costs include approximately $250 per veteran's hunt day and approximately $500 per hunt weekend.

Is my donation tax-deductible?

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization. Donations may be tax-deductible as allowed by law.

How is NPVF different from other veteran nonprofits?

NPVF focuses on outdoor fellowship programs built around hunting, conservation work, and community connection — meaningful time outdoors at no cost to participants.

Who runs NPVF?

NPVF is led by Founder & CEO Steven Vaca. The leadership team includes Cory Northup (Vice President), Caitlyn Vaca (Treasurer), John Calhoun (Chief Operating Officer), Jo Stalnaker (Director of Veterans), and Saul Grosshuesch (Director of Marketing & Communications).

How can companies partner with NPVF?

Companies can support NPVF through partnerships that expand fully funded outdoor fellowship opportunities. Partnership levels include Shoveler ($1,000), Redhead ($2,500), Bluebill ($5,000), and Canada Goose ($10,000).

How can I volunteer with NPVF?

Volunteers support the mission by contributing time, skills, or resources to outdoor fellowship programs — event support, logistics, conservation projects, and more.

Can I donate gear instead of money?

Yes — NPVF welcomes conversations about in-kind donations of gear or services that support the mission.

How do I contact NPVF?

NPVF is based in Belton, Missouri, serving the Kansas City region. Reach us through our contact page.
