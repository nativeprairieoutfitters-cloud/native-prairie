One prairie. Two missions.

Native Prairie is home to two separate organizations: a nonprofit foundation providing fully funded outdoor fellowships for veterans, active-duty service members, and Gold Star families — and a professional guided-hunting outfitter. Choose your path below.

Native Prairie Veterans Foundation

A Missouri 501(c)(3) nonprofit providing fully funded outdoor fellowship programs — hunting, conservation, and community — for those who served and their families. Donations are tax-deductible as allowed by law.

Visit the Foundation

Native Prairie Outfitters LLC

Professional guided hunting in Missouri, Kansas, and Iowa — waterfowl, turkey, archery deer, and dove on roughly 2,000 leased acres. Built around ADA accessibility.

Visit the Outfitter

Legally separate: Native Prairie Veterans Foundation is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization (EIN 42-2391986), legally separate from Native Prairie Outfitters LLC, a for-profit business. Both are led by Steven Vaca. Donations to the Foundation remain Foundation assets — any payment to the Outfitter requires documented fair-value services and disinterested board approval.

The Foundation's work so far

NPVF has served five veterans through fully funded fellowships since our founding in 2026, and our goal is to serve twenty veterans by the end of 2026 and forty in 2027. Fellowships run on private partner land in Cass County, Missouri, always with the landowner's written permission, and participants pay nothing for the hunt, the guiding, or the ground. A single veteran's hunt day costs about $250, and every dollar donated goes toward getting veterans outdoors.

How to help

You can support the mission by donating through our Hunt for a Hero campaign, volunteering on a hunt day or conservation project, sponsoring a fellowship, or simply spreading the word. Visit the Foundation's Ways to Give page to donate, or get in touch to volunteer.
